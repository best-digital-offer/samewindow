How PriceSnap Works
01 / CAPTURE

Snap or upload any product

Take a snapshot with your mobile camera or upload a screenshot. Images are compressed locally inside your browser for instant, private processing.

02 / IDENTIFY

Active AI vision analysis

Groq vision scans distinctive shape cues, logos, model markings, and packaging details with auto-recovery across verified active vision models.

03 / WORLDWIDE SEARCH

Real shopping links anywhere

Instant deep-search links across online stores in the US, UK, Europe, India, Canada, Australia, Japan, and worldwide. Zero fake prices or mock data.
